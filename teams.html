The programs

Three teams,
One community.

This Boosters Club supports local robotics teams to ensure they have the funding, materials, and community backing to succeed and grow, from light plastic robots to industrial-size machines.



Learn more about our teams:

01

Ridgely Middle School · VEX IQ

Ridgely Robotics

The robotics club at Ridgely Middle school engages over 80 students in the VEX IQ Robotics Competition. Students design, build and code their own robots and compete in collaborative tournaments across the state.

Visit Ridgely Robotics ↗
02

Dulaney High School · VEX V5

1727 REX - VEX Robotics

The VEX Robotics Club at Dulaney High School participates in the VEX V5 Robotics Competition. Students design, build, and code their own metal robots to compete in fast-paced, two-vs-two matches in Maryland and beyond.

Meet 1727 VEX↗
03

Dulaney High School · FIRST Robotics Competition

1727 REX - FRC

The FIRST Robotics Club at Dulaney High School participates in the FIRST Robotics Competition. Students collaborate to create one industrial sized robot to compete in a gym sized arena in three-versus-three matches across the region.
