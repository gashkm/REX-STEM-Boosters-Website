Why join?

Membership benefits you, and our teams

For members

Membership benefits

  • Stay informed

    Receive updates about teams, events, competitions, meetings, and volunteer opportunities.

  • Have a voice

    Join our meetings as a voting member to advocate for your teams and community STEM initiatives.

  • Attend events

    Receive invitations to our community events such as the STEM career speaker series.

  • Build connections

    Be connected to the current and alumni network of Ridgely and Dulaney robotics teams.

For our programs

What membership supports

  • All our teams!

    The collective needs of all of our teams, providing shared resources like concessions equipment, fundraising coordination and online/social media outreach.

  • Outreach and new initiatives

    New STEM community initiatives like introductory robotics workshops for girl scout troops and elementary students.

  • Student recognition

    Student acknowledgements like senior recognition and end of season celebrations.

Who can join?

Anyone!

Parents, grandparents, extended family, robotics alumni, interested community members...we welcome everyone to support our STEM leaders of tomorrow!
